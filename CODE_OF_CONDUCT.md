# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Seriee is a small, volunteer-run project. We want participation to be a safe and
welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background,
identity, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code, not people.
- Keep discussions on topic and in the appropriate issue or pull request.
- Respect privacy: do not publish another person's private information.
- Follow the licenses and terms of the data sources the app relies on.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, hidden telemetry, or content that
  infringes copyright.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and review comments) and when representing the project in
public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately through the repository's **Security** tab or by
contacting the maintainer listed in the README. Reports are handled
confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or
temporarily or permanently ban participants. Enforcement decisions will be
proportional to the behavior and its impact.
